Four cheap models, one boring result Chaar saste model, ek boring result

We gave five small jobs to the cheap models on opencode-go. Every one of them got every job right. So the only things left to compare are price and speed. opencode-go ke saste models ko paanch chhote kaam diye. Sabne sab sahi kiya. Ab compare karne ke liye sirf daam aur speed bache.

01

the setupWhat we did

opencode-go is a service that serves models. You send it words, a model sends words back, and you pay for the size of the words that moved — counted in .

Models change often and prices move, so we keep a small and run it now and then. Today we took four models and gave each one five small jobs. Every job has a right answer, so we could simply count how many each model got.

Small test, not a leaderboard. Five jobs cannot describe a whole model. Read this as a smoke check, not a verdict on how smart anything is.

03

the workThe five jobs

The first two are easy. The last three are built to trip a model up.

  • Three quick questions. A bat and a ball, the trailing zeros in 100!, and counting a letter in a word.
  • Short code. Find the longest run of letters with no repeats.
  • A calculator. Read maths the way people write it — brackets, plus, minus, times, divide, and a minus sign in front.
  • A tiny pattern matcher. Two rules only: a dot matches one letter, a star repeats whatever came before it.
  • Five harder questions. The last digit of 3 to the power 2026, numbers with exactly three divisors, and so on.

Each job was run once per model, from the same prompt. The answers were checked by running them on the machine, not by reading them.

the pointWhat we'd pick

  • 🟡Quality — no winner today. Four models, five jobs, every answer right.
  • 🟢Speed — DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, clearly, once the job gets heavy.
  • 🔵Price — Muse Spark 1.3, at a third of the output cost.
  • 🔒Privacy — DeepSeek does not train on your data. Muse does, and it asks first.

If it has to be one line: keep DeepSeek V4.1 Flash as the default, and reach for Muse Spark 1.3 when the bill matters more than the data.

01

setupHumne kya kiya

opencode-go ek service hai jo models deti hai. Tum kuch bhejte ho, model jawab deta hai, aur tum words ke size ka paisa dete ho — jise kehte hain.

Models badalte rehte hain aur daam hilte rehte hain, isliye hum ek chhota rakhte hain aur beech-beech me chalate hain. Aaj humne chaar model liye aur har ek ko paanch chhote kaam diye. Har kaam ka ek sahi jawab hai, isliye seedha ginti kar sakte hain.

Chhota test, leaderboard nahi. Paanch kaam se poora model describe nahi hota. Ise smoke check samjho, dimaag ka faisla nahi.

03

kaamPaanch kaam

Pehle do aasan hain. Aakhri teen jaan-boojh kar model ko phasane ke liye banaye.

  • Teen chhote sawaal. Bat aur ball, 100! ke aakhir me zeros, aur ek word me letter ginti.
  • Chhota code. Aisi sabse lambi letters ki kataar dhoondo jisme koi letter repeat na ho.
  • Ek calculator. Maths waise padho jaise log likhte hain — brackets, plus, minus, times, divide, aur aage minus sign.
  • Chhota pattern matcher. Sirf do niyam: dot ek letter se match karta hai, star pichhli cheez repeat karta hai.
  • Paanch mushkil sawaal. 3 ki power 2026 ka aakhri digit, theek teen divisor wale number, waghairah.

Har kaam har model pe ek baar chala. Jawab machine pe chala kar check kiye, aankh se nahi.
